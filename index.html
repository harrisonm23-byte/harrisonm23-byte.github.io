About

I study how AI agents behave under economic pressure, and what that means for markets and for governance.

I work on model evaluations at SpaceXAI. I write evaluation items and grading rubrics for Grok, and review other graders’ scoring. My latest paper, A Push Down the Mountain, tests whether framed messages push a long-horizon trading agent toward collusion, and whether steering its internal state changes that.

Research interests: agent safety evaluations, interpretability and steering, the macroeconomics of AI and technical AI governance.

    Harrison C. Margolin

    Working manuscript, October 2026
